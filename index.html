🧭 Identification Framework ▾
How trends and bottlenecks are identified — and why asymmetric positioning matters more than fundamentals

1 · Trend → Bottleneck Mapping

We identify 2–4 year mega-trends, then locate the physical or structural bottleneck each trend creates. The company that solves or enables the bottleneck captures disproportionate value. Example: AI training needs power → grid is the bottleneck → nuclear SMR companies win.

2 · Quantitative Screening

  • Market Cap < $35B — asymmetric upside room
  • Cash Runway > 12 months — avoids dilution risk
  • Cash / Market Cap > 8% — balance sheet margin of safety
  • Revenue Growth momentum — acceleration = PMF signal
  • Gross Margins — high margins = software-like scalability
  • Beta, Short Interest — volatility and contrarian signals

3 · Qualitative Scoring

  • TAM Growth (10%): Is the addressable market expanding?
  • Partnership (12%): Hyperscaler / Defense Prime relationships = de-risked pipeline
  • Innovation (12%): R&D intensity + technology pivot optionality
  • Scalability (10%): Can they fulfill large orders? Margin structure?

⚠️ What Is This Section?

These companies do not yet meet all screening criteria — they may be pre-revenue, burning cash, or simply too small for institutional coverage. That is exactly the point. The goal is to identify before institutional discovery: low float, low volume stocks that are directly positioned at a structural bottleneck. When a hyperscaler partnership, government contract, or regulatory milestone hits, institutions arrive simultaneously — and the stock reprices violently upward.
📦 High Backlog / Market-Cap Ratio ▾
Backlog (or RPO) ≥ 30% of market cap — signals locked-in revenue growth and adoption inflection in the next 1–3 years

💡 Why This Matters

A company sitting on backlog several times its market cap has already sold revenue the market has not yet priced in. As that backlog converts, revenue accelerates, margins scale, and re-rating follows. This screen flags companies where the ratio implies disproportionate forward growth versus current enterprise value. Backlog figures are sourced from the latest 10-K / 10-Q / earnings call — ratios update live with market cap.
📞 Abnormal Call Volume — Bullish Options Flow ▾
Nearest-expiry call volume ≥ 0.5× open interest with skew bullish (P/C < 0.75) — often precedes upside moves or earnings beats

⚡ Why Smart Money Watches This

When today's call volume blows past existing open interest and the put/call ratio is skewed bullish, someone is positioning aggressively for an imminent move. Common triggers: pending earnings beat, contract announcement, regulatory milestone, M&A rumor. Live nearest-expiry option chains are pulled per refresh — read this as a positioning signal, not a recommendation. Always confirm with fundamentals.

🔥 Short Squeeze Setup (+4 to +10) — high short% with momentum building.
📅 Earnings Imminent (+6 to +8) — within ±14 days. Volatility expansion alone is asymmetric.
⭐ Analyst Consensus (+5 to +8) — institutional cover with positive bias.
📊 52W Multi-Bagger (+5) — already-in-motion stories tend to stay in motion.

📉 Off Screener — Performance Tracking ▾

📊 Read This Like a Box Score

Every time a ticker leaves the screener, we record the exit price and keep pulling its live price forever. If dropped tickers fall after exit, the screener earned its keep. If they rip higher, the screener probably let go too early. Δ Since Exit is color-coded that way: green = ticker fell (screener was right), red = ticker rallied (missed move).
